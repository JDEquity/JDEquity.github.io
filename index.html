About Entrepreneurship & AI

Our mission is to prepare aspirational young people to be productive, capable and confident members of their communities. We aim to provide underserved youth with the resources which may be neccessary to reach their aspirational goals - all within a a safe and inclusive AI environment.

Dean Griffiths is a knowledgable educator and IT specialist. He has worked as a Senior Analyst Programmer for IBM GSA and and a Leading teacher in Secondary Education. He has taught at a variety of institutions, including Balwyn High School, Brentwood SC, Preston Girls SC, Wesley College and Melbourne High School. Dean has coordinated and developed curriculum initiatives spanning Junior and Senior VCE classes. He has taught PE, VCE Mathematics, Physics, Software Development and HESS Algorithmics. Dean has a Bachelor of Education with major studies in PE for the Handicapped and Graduate Diplomas in Computing and Mathematics. With over 20 years experience as a teacher, Dean is an experienced, dynamic and trusted educator who values an inclusive approach to the education of young people.

Joseph Marotta is the Head of Commerce at Melbourne High School, one of Victoria's leading select-entry state schools, where he has coordinated the Economics, Legal Studies, Business Management, Accounting and Civics curriculum for over three decades. Beyond the classroom, he is an active voice in business education, engaging with organisations such as the Victorian Commercial Teachers Association and collaborating on entrepreneurship and leadership initiatives for students, including AI-in-education forums and pro-bono workshops. Joe is an educationalist, author, and businessman, reflecting interests that extend beyond teaching into writing and Entrepreneurship.
